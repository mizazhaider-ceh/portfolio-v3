## 3. Pinterest search method and its limits

**Search direction from both example pins:** search Pinterest for more videos and visuals in these two moods, pixel-art calm motion on one side, bright calm focused work on the other. Prefer calm, editorial, print-like motion that fits Monograph, no cheesy hacker imagery, no green terminal neon.

**How the search was done:** text search only (`browser.search`), plus direct fetch attempts on the two example pins (both 403, see section 2). No login was used. No Pinterest media was downloaded. Pinterest pin pages generally block text fetch without login (403), so most candidates below are recorded from the search index (title, snippet, idea page or pin URL, and any duration the index shows), not from a full pin page read. That is a real limit, and it is the reason every candidate below is marked NOT usable until its rights are verified on the pin page and on the original source.

**What that limit means for rights:** Pinterest pins are very often reposts with unclear rights. A pin title and a thumbnail do not establish who made the work, what licence it carries, or whether the pinner had the right to post it. The licence checklist in `docs/02-ux-video-research.md` section B3 requires opening the licence page for the specific clip, confirming commercial use and modification, checking every frame for a face, logo, or readable screen, and recording the source, licence name, clip URL, and download date beside the file. None of that could be completed for any Pinterest candidate in this pass, because the pin pages did not open and no original source page was linked from the search snippets. Every candidate is therefore recorded as rights unclear, NOT usable, unless its row says otherwise. No candidate in this pass has clearly verified free rights.

**Counts, honestly:** 8 Pinterest finds are ranked in section 4 (5 video or animation finds, 3 idea pages or collections that contain videos). Of those 8, 0 have verified free rights, 0 have a verified original source with a free licence, and 0 were downloaded. Two further original-source leads found through the same searches (section 5) also fail the rights check, one explicitly (repost not allowed) and one by ownership (university work). The shortlist in section 4 is therefore a mood and direction record, not a shipping shortlist.
